# BANT changelog

## Version 2 (2026, Mendeley Data Version 2 in preparation)

- Contributors: added Mohammad Shamsul Arefin, Hamid Hosen, Riadul Islam Rabbi, and Md Jakir Hossen to the dataset record.
- De-identification: 10 comments still contained personal identifiers that the Version 1 screen missed. These were 7 phone numbers (several written in Bengali digits), 1 e-mail address, 2 Facebook Messenger links, and 1 raw news URL. They were replaced with `<PHONE>`, `<EMAIL>`, and `<URL>` in the Bengali, English, and bilingual files of both task views.
- Unchanged: row count, row order, labels, fine-grained categories, sources, and train/validation/test membership.
